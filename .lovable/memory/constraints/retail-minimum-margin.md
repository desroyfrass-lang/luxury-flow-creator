name: Retail minimum 25% margin
description: Every FRASS KICKS retail sale keeps at least 25% profit margin after all selling costs; commission 10% is separate; no retail sales allocation set
type: constraint
---
- Every retail product sale must keep at least 25% PROFIT MARGIN (profit ÷ charged price, not markup) after supplier cost, shipping paid by FRASS, processing, packaging, discounts and other real costs.
- Missing costs = no price. Unsafe prices are blocked before any write.
- The 10% figure is for COMMISSIONS, not a retail sales cut.
- Retail sales allocation/platform cut is NOT established — never invent 8% or 10% for retail.
- affiliate_policy.platform_allocation_rate (8) belongs to the affiliate engine only.
