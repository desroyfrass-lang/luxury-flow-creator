name: Retail minimum 25% margin
description: Every FRASS KICKS retail sale keeps at least 25% profit margin after all selling costs; commission 10% is separate; no retail sales allocation set
type: constraint
---
- Every retail product sale must keep at least 25% PROFIT MARGIN (profit ÷ charged price, not markup) after supplier cost, shipping paid by FRASS, processing, packaging, discounts and other real costs.
- Missing costs = no price. Unsafe prices are blocked before any write.
- The 10% figure is for COMMISSIONS, not a retail sales cut.
- Retail sales allocation/platform cut is NOT established — never invent 8% or 10% for retail.
- affiliate_policy.platform_allocation_rate (8) belongs to the affiliate engine only.
- Target range (Founder, 2026-10-11): 40% preferred starting target, 30–39.99% acceptable, 25–29.99% needs Founder review, <25% blocked. 40% is a target, never a floor. Recommend a price only when every cost is verified; otherwise show lower-bound estimates labelled not safe/approved.
- Shipping (Founder, 2026-10-11, replaces customer-pays default): "Standard Shipping Included" — standard delivery is built into each country/zone retail price and is a FRASS cost in the margin; customers are not charged shipping again at checkout. Oversized/heavy items and unsupported destinations need a quote/exception. Never invent shipping amounts; missing or stale CJ quotes fail closed. Not live for shoppers until each zone is verified and the Founder enables it.
