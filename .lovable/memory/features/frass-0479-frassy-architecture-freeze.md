# FRASS-0479 — Frassy Architecture Freeze

**Status:** Constitutional. **Priority:** Permanent. **Effective:** Immediately.

Frassy has reached architectural maturity. From this point forward, Frassy is
not an experimental feature — she is the operating system of the Frass
ecosystem. Her foundation is now constitutionally frozen.

## Frozen Systems

These are stable architecture. They may be optimized internally but must
never be duplicated or fundamentally redesigned without a Founder
Constitutional Amendment:

1. **One Frassy** — there is only one Frassy. She exists everywhere. She
   never becomes another AI; she simply understands where she is and changes
   her responsibilities accordingly.
2. **One Shared Chat Engine**
3. **One Shared Voice Engine** — cloud → device → text hierarchy, never silence.
4. **One Shared Conversation History** — changing rooms never restarts the
   conversation; refreshing never resets it.
5. **One Shared Personality** — warm, intelligent, professional, encouraging,
   calm, naturally humorous. Never robotic, never theatrical. Only her
   responsibilities change, never her identity.
6. **One Shared Presence Engine** — welcome once, welcome back on return,
   quiet while working, respect Focus Mode, one idle offer, never interrupt
   productive work, never disappear unexpectedly, never remain silently broken.
7. **One Shared Context Engine** — where am I, who am I speaking to, what
   permissions do they have, what is this room for, should this be shared.
8. **One Shared Working Style Engine** — learns voice/text, detail/concise,
   work hours, reminder prefs, pacing, interruptions. Lives on device, visible,
   resettable. Never changes personality, only improves assistance.
9. **One Shared Memory Layer**
10. **One Shared Permission Layer**

Every room extends these systems. No room owns its own copy.

## Future Development — Allowed

Future updates MAY improve:

- Business knowledge, marketplace knowledge, financial explanations
- Founder reporting, business coaching
- Conversation quality, timing, explanations
- Accessibility, performance, language support
- New departments and new business specializations

## Future Development — Forbidden

Future updates must NOT:

- Duplicate Frassy or create department-specific assistants
- Create independent memories, personalities, voice engines, or chat engines
- Fork or fundamentally redesign the frozen core

## Extension Principle

Every future Frass department teaches Frassy something new. Nothing creates
another Frassy. The architecture never changes — only Frassy's knowledge
expands.

Examples: Frass University teaches Frassy education. Children's World teaches
child-safe interaction. Travel teaches travel planning. Real Estate teaches
property guidance. Healthcare teaches approved wellness information.

## Founder Closing Statement

Frassy's foundation is complete. From this point forward, focus shifts away
from rebuilding the assistant and toward building the world she guides people
through. This freeze marks the transition from platform construction to
platform refinement, preparing Frass for launch with a single, consistent
intelligence at its heart.
