**FRASS-0423 — locked.**

- **Welcome Hall** is the *registration and arrival gateway* to Frass Hill (public, `/welcome-hall`). The authenticated builder dashboard lives at `/builder-hall`.
- **Town Square** is a *feature inside Frass Hill* you reach after registering — a directory of real presences with quarters (Performance Corner, Business Row, Farmers' Corner, Wedding Expo, Builders' Yard, Foundation Steps, Artists' Arcade, Notice Wall).
- **Children never walk through Town Square.** They take **Kids Valley** (`/kids-valley`) down to Children's Village. Entering Frass Hill offers two roads: Frass Hill or Kids Valley.
- **Cinematic Standard:** photorealistic, real people, real places. No cartoon/illustrated imagery anywhere on Frass Hill. Exceptions kept: the Founder's husband hero, the Bob Marley graffiti murals, and the kids sections.
- **For Me → About is a Living Bio:** an always-editable page separate from posts — biography, story, mission, business, foundation work, websites/business/social links, portfolio, photos, videos, skills, interests, achievements, contact preference. Stored in `profiles.about` (jsonb), edited via `ForMeAbout`.
