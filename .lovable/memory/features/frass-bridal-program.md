Bridal is a **destination**, not a collection. A wedding is a journey, not a
purchase. Reserve the name **"The Frass Bridal Experience"** for the complete
ecosystem; the shop is one chapter within it.

Entry path: Frass Luxury House → Bridal → choose your journey.

## Journeys (never mix these collections)
Bride (gowns, reception dresses, shoes, jewelry, accessories, veils, beauty,
preparation) · Groom (tuxedos, suits, footwear, accessories, formalwear, styling) ·
Bridesmaids · Groomsmen · Flower Girl · Ring Bearer · Mother of the Bride ·
Mother of the Groom · Wedding Guests.

## Ecosystem services
- **Wedding Registry** — every couple gets one; guests purchase directly; integrated
  across Frass.
- **Wedding Marketplace** — verified photographers, florists, decorators, cake
  artists, musicians, venues, transportation, invitations, beauty pros, planners.
- **Honeymoon** — luxury travel, resort fashion, luggage, travel accessories.
- **Gifts** — wedding, anniversary, keepsakes, personalised, home collections.
- **Family Vision Maps** — shared couple maps: wedding budget, first home,
  honeymoon, future family, business together, community goals. Marriage as the
  start of a shared Builder Journey.
- **Community** — optional sharing, milestones, mentoring future couples.
- **Editorial** — magazine feel: real weddings, designer features, planning
  inspiration, cultural traditions, seasonal trends, luxury photography.

## Cultural inclusion
Caribbean, African, Indian, Asian, European, Latin American, Middle Eastern,
Indigenous, interfaith, modern and traditional ceremonies. Every couple represented.

## Environment
Romantic: elegant gardens, stone pathways, water features, glass conservatories,
wedding pavilions, blooming flowers, golden-hour lighting, soft orchestral music
(future phase). Luxury without excess.

## Product population buckets
Bride · Groom · Bridesmaids · Groomsmen · Children · Guests · Accessories ·
Beauty · Decor · Registry. Coherent hierarchy, never mixed.

## Cross-district connections (wedding operating system)
Luxury House → fashion · Marketplace → vendors and services · Builder Vault →
planning documents · Opportunity Center → wedding business opportunities ·
Academy → courses for wedding professionals · Foundation → community initiatives
and cultural preservation · Vision Maps → financial planning for the wedding and beyond.
