Bridal is a **destination**, not a collection. A wedding is a journey, not a
purchase. Reserve the name **"The Frass Bridal Experience"** for the complete
ecosystem; the shop is one chapter within it.

**FRASS-0930:** Bridal no longer lives inside Luxury House. **Frass Bridal is
its own district in Frass Hill**, physically beside Frass Luxury House (a gate
links them). Routes: `/bridal` village · `/bridal/journey` · `/bridal/vault` ·
`/bridal/collections` · `/bridal/sourcing` · `/bridal/marketplace`.

## Journeys (never mix these collections)
Bride · Groom · Bridesmaids · Groomsmen · Flower Girl · Ring Bearer ·
Mother of the Bride · Mother of the Groom · Wedding Guests.

## Ecosystem services
- **Wedding Vault** — budget, timeline, 35-item smart checklist, partner
  details, family vision. Local-first, resumable.
- **Dress Collaboration** — save ~40 dresses, one secure share link, virtual
  try-on, party voting, narrowing rounds 40 → 15 → 8 → 5 → 3 → 1. Every round
  stays saved.
- **Sourcing Desk** — a dress Frass doesn't carry becomes a sourcing case:
  Requested → Vendor Contact → Marketplace Review → Supplier Approved →
  Product Created → Available. Every request grows the marketplace.
- **Wedding Marketplace** — attire, flowers, photography, cake, music, venues,
  transport, invitations, beauty, planners. Bridal vendors are appointment
  vendors: portfolios, availability, quotes, contracts.
- **Wedding Pricing Intelligence** — compare on quality, timeline, alterations,
  shipping, returns — not price alone.
- **Registry · Honeymoon · Gifts**.
- **Family Vision Maps** — wedding budget, first home, honeymoon, future family,
  business together, community goals. Marriage as the start of a shared Builder
  Journey.
- **Editorial** — real weddings, designers, cultural traditions, seasonal trends.

## FRASS-0931 Frassy Wedding Concierge
