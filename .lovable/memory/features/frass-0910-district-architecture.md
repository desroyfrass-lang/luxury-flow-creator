# FRASS-0910 — District Functional Architecture (Volume X)

Districts are living environments, not decoration. Every district must answer: **what happens here?**
If that cannot be answered in one sentence, the district is incomplete.

## Terminology standard (constitutional)
- **Frass Builders** — anyone building a business, project or opportunity ON the platform.
- **Skilled Builders** — tradespeople who physically build communities (carpenters, masons, electricians, plumbers, roofers, welders, engineers, contractors).
- The bare word "Builder" is never used alone in Frass Hill from FRASS-0910 forward.

## The eight universal rules
Emotional identity · Functional purpose · Primary audience · Connected systems · Daily activity · Seasonal evolution · Community contribution · Legacy.

## The districts
Children's Village · Community Square · Builder Academy · Opportunity Centre · Music Quarter ·
DJ District · Frass Builders Village (skilled trades) · Farm Hub · Foundation District ·
Marketplace Pavilion · Reflection Gardens · Founder Hall.

Economic engines: Marketplace Pavilion, Frass Builders Village, DJ District, Music Quarter, Farm Hub.

## District Stewards
Frassy adapts professional expertise per district while keeping one personality:
educator (Children's Village), host (Community Square), teacher (Academy), career advisor
(Opportunity Centre), producer (Music Quarter), performance coach and booking agent (DJ District),
master tradesperson and construction business consultant (Builders Village), agricultural
consultant (Farm Hub), programme director (Foundation), merchandising officer (Marketplace),
quiet counsel (Reflection Gardens), chief of staff (Founder Hall).

## Cross-district journeys
Districts must never feel isolated: growing-up walk, land-to-table walk, trades walk, sound walk.
