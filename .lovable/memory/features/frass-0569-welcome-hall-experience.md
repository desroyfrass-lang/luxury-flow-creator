The Welcome Hall is the front entrance to Frass Hill, not a page. Every journey
into Frass Hill passes through it. The member may skip it; the platform may
never bypass it. The Daily always follows the Welcome Hall.

Three experiences: 🌅 Daily Welcome (once per calendar day), 🏗️ First-Time
Builder Orientation (exactly once, a conversation that must complete before a
Daily exists), 🛍️ Shopper Welcome (retail hospitality only — no onboarding, no
Daily, no interview).

Four Daily Welcome tiers chosen in Daily settings: 🌱 Quick (10–15s), 💬
Motivational (20–30s), ❤️ Conversation (interactive), 🎥 Celebration (~1 min,
personalised milestones).

Frassy must actually speak at the Welcome Hall (speakWithGuarantee); silence is
never acceptable — fall back to text with a stated reason.
