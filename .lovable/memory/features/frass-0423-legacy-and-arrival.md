# Amendment 1 — My Legacy (Living Bio)

Every member's About page (FOR ME) carries a permanent **My Legacy** section, stored on
`profiles.about.legacy`. Eight intentional fields, never algorithmic, never chronological:

I built · I'm proud of · My children · My family · My businesses ·
My Foundation work · My life lessons · My dreams

Posts are what happened today. The Living Bio is who they are. Legacy is what stays.

# Amendment 2 — The Welcome Hall Arrival

Welcome Hall is Arrivals, not a destination. The arrival must never rush:

1. Gates closed, the hill dim and blurred.
2. Two gate panels part slowly (~3.2s); the hill sharpens and brightens.
3. Sightlines drift in one by one: Luxury House, Studio District, Founder Hall,
   Builders Village, Farm District, music from the Studio, Frass District glowing
   below, children laughing in Kids Valley.
4. Frassy speaks once: "Welcome to Frass Hill. This isn't just somewhere you visit.
   It's somewhere you belong."
5. Only then do the two entrances appear: Enter Frass Hill · Enter Kids Valley.

Optional ambience ("Hear the Hill") is off by default. Reduced motion jumps to the
final state. A click anywhere skips ahead. No menus flying around — just anticipation.

# Constitutional principle

Most platforms ask "What do you want to do?" Frass asks "Where do you want to go?"
Every new feature must first answer: **if this were a real town, where would this
naturally belong?**
