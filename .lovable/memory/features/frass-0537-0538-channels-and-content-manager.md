**FRASS-0537 Meet Members Where They Are (P0).** Members never learn new technology
to use Frass. Frassy reaches them on the Frass website, Frass mobile app, WhatsApp,
ChatGPT, Claude, email, SMS where appropriate and supported voice integrations —
one identity, one memory, one experience across all of them.

WhatsApp is a first-class channel (primary platform for many Caribbean members).
Where technically supported: receive the Daily, text Frassy, send voice notes,
receive voice replies, review Money Moves, update Blueprints, get reminders,
continue Business Vault work, complete onboarding — all inside WhatsApp.

Honesty boundary: never promise free or guaranteed telephone connectivity (e.g.
free WhatsApp calls to landlines). Frassy instead prepares the member for the call,
finds the number, drafts what to say, initiates supported channels, tracks
follow-ups.

**FRASS-0538 Frassy Content Manager (P0).** Content is edited by conversation.
Frassy may manage text, images, collections, capsules, marketing copy, landing
pages, Welcome Hall content, Daily messages and knowledge articles — every write
still passes FRASS-0539 approval. Engineering is reserved for application logic,
database structure, authentication, security, APIs, business rules and performance.

Planned permission expansion (all Founder-approved): marketing publishing,
marketplace product/pricing changes, Knowledge Vault organisation, Business Vault
lessons and templates, Founder Mode navigation/onboarding/Blueprint templates.

Lovable is the software engineer. Frassy is the operations manager.
