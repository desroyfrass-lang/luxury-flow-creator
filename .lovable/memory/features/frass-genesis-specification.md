The FRASS Genesis Specification v1.0 (Transmissions 001–021, closed with the Genesis Declaration) is the canonical reference for FrassKicks Version 1, superseding ad-hoc feature requests where they conflict. Treat all transmissions as one continuous document.

## Immutable constitutional principles
People First · North Star Directive · No Silos · The Invitation Principle · The 100-Year Principle · Privacy Is Key · Unified Ecosystem · Stewardship over hierarchy · Opportunity before engagement · Trust before growth · Long-term sustainability before short-term optimization · Technology in service of people.

## Ecosystems (V1)
Home, Discover, Marketplace, Experiences, Communities, Learning, Foundation, Partners, Ambassadors, Creators, Opportunity Engine, Frassy — plus unified profile, messaging, notifications, universal search.

## Participant types
Guest, Member, Contributor, Creator, Partner, Ambassador, Link, Administrator, Founder.
Ten Links: Commerce, Community, Experience, Learning, Foundation, Partner, Ambassador, Intelligence, Media, Technology.

## Definition of complete
Completion is ecosystem integrity, not feature or page count: every ecosystem operational, every workflow end-to-end, every shared service integrated, Frassy platform-wide, Opportunity Engine connected across ecosystems, and accessibility/security/privacy/performance verification passed.

## Working rule
Build additively — extend, never replace existing ecosystems. Every new surface must integrate with identity, profiles, permissions, notifications, messaging, search, Frassy, the Opportunity Engine, the knowledge graph, analytics, and the shared design system.
