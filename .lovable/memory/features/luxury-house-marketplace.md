Frass Luxury House is a curated luxury **marketplace**, not a single-owner retailer.
It combines Frass-owned collections, curated marketplace brands, independent
luxury designers, boutique fashion houses, artisan creators, Caribbean luxury
brands, and international premium partners. Goal: the world's most thoughtfully
curated luxury marketplace.

## Three ownership models (every product is exactly one)
1. **Frass Collection** — designed and owned by Frass; Frass inventory; Frass branding.
2. **Marketplace Partner** — owned by an independent partner, sold through Luxury
   House, partner fulfils, revenue split by marketplace rules.
3. **Curated Luxury Brand** — established designer houses, ateliers, premium
   boutiques; Frass is the marketplace host.

## Product identity (internal, never customer-facing)
Every product record must resolve: owner · fulfiller · shipper · payee ·
affiliate commission recipient · accounting model · tax treatment · return policy.

## Customer experience
Unified. One cart, one checkout, one luxury experience. The customer never feels
they left Frass.

## Frassy's rule during population
Always ask: Frass product, Marketplace product, or Curated Partner product?
Ownership determines fulfilment, accounting, commissions and reporting.
Never assume ownership — always classify. Marketplace/curated products are NOT
dropship products and follow the partner accounting path.
