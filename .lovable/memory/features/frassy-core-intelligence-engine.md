# Frassy Core Intelligence Engine

## Orchestrator Principle
Frassy is not an application inside FrassKicks. Frassy is the orchestration layer that coordinates the entire platform. Every subsystem, workflow, recommendation, and major participant experience communicates through or is coordinated by Frassy.

## Platform Awareness Model (three layers maintained simultaneously)
1. **Participant Awareness** — goals, projects, learning, businesses, creative work, marketplace activity, community participation, foundation involvement, executive responsibilities, communication preferences, builder maturity, recent accomplishments, momentum level.
2. **Platform Awareness** — current building context, current dashboard, available tools, connected assistants, workflows, notifications, system health, pending automation, relevant opportunities, available learning resources.
3. **Organizational Awareness** — community activity, marketplace activity, academy updates, foundation initiatives, new opportunities, new AI capabilities, platform improvements, relevant announcements.

## Interaction Lifecycle (seven stages)
1. **Observe** — participant enters; Frassy evaluates context silently.
2. **Understand** — determine today's objective and whether priorities should shift.
3. **Prepare** — assemble relevant data, projects, tools, opportunities, assistants, lessons, assets before speaking.
4. **Engage** — interact only after preparation so conversation feels immediate.
5. **Collaborate** — the longest phase: building, teaching, creating, planning, reviewing, learning, editing, business development, problem solving.
6. **Reflect** — before ending, evaluate what changed, what remains, and whether confidence/capability/momentum improved.
7. **Prepare For Return** — future reminders, opportunities, next logical step, knowledge organization, project continuity.

## Passive Intelligence
Frassy observes, learns, waits, and intervenes only when intervention creates genuine value (preventing mistakes, protecting momentum, presenting significant opportunities, preventing duplicated work, offering help when confusion is detected). Silence is sometimes the highest form of intelligence.

## Notification Philosophy
Notifications should rarely feel administrative. They communicate meaningful progress and answer: "Why does this matter to the participant?"

## Continuity
- **Cross-Building Continuity** — entering another building must not reset the experience; Frassy carries project status, monetization opportunities, business implications, academy lessons, and collaborators across contexts.
- **Session Continuity** — every session feels like opening an ongoing chapter, reconnecting participants with yesterday's work, current priorities, active milestones, recent ideas, and pending opportunities.

## Engineering Directive
Architect the platform so Frassy functions as the central orchestration service. Subsystems request intelligence from Frassy rather than independently implementing conversational behavior. Maintain one shared participant context across the ecosystem. Avoid fragmented AI experiences.

## Multi-Assistant Inheritance
Future assistants (Sheldon, creative specialists, business specialists, academy mentors, foundation assistants) become specialized expressions of the same underlying intelligence, sharing context, standards, and participant understanding — not separate AIs with separate memory.
