FRASS-0421 Phase 2. Founder approved.

**Town Square (`/town-square`)** — NOT TikTok. TikTok is people; Town Square is people, businesses, artists, brands, farmers, builders, events, stores, districts, live broadcasts, community, foundation, announcements and creators. It is a directory of *presences*, not a feed of posts. Everything on Frass Hill starts here, and it connects For Us, Go Live, Frass Radio, Viral and every For Me page. Registry: `src/lib/town-square.ts` (presence lenses + permanent stalls + notice board + hourly square mood). Nav "Town Square" points here (no longer `/welcome-hall`).

**For Me (`/for-me`)** — one Builder's own page. Never "moments chosen for you"; nothing algorithmic. Fixed sections, in order: Hero Video, Bio, Stories, Posts, Photos, Videos, Collections, Saved, Followers, Following, Achievements, Creator Journey, Media, About, Message. Everything about me.

Approved Phase 1 decisions to keep: For Us typography is readable (Manrope), chrome glow replaces gold for interaction (gold is reserved for luxury moments), Frassy has a living beacon (glow/blink/breathing, never distracting), Viral is the platform-wide word, Liquidation Room is a discovered store not a nav item.
