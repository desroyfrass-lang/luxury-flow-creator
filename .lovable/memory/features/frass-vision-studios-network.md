## One company, two expressions
- **Frass Vision Studios** — official/legal/public name. Credits, contracts, marketing. "A Frass Vision Studios Original."
- **FV Studios** — everyday ecosystem shorthand. "Created in FV Studios." Always plural.
- Never present them as two companies, two brands, or two businesses. Same house.
- Everyday work = FV Studios. Big work (song, album, film, documentary, series) = Frass Vision Studios.

## Business model (non-negotiable)
- Royalties are tied to **publishing through the Frass Vision Network**, never to using the editor.
- Private/family/demo/self-released work: 0% to Frass, always.
- Published & monetised through Frass: agreed, published participation percentage. No hidden cuts, full line-item receipts.
- Default participation: recordings 15%, publishing admin 10%, film licensing 20%, podcast 15%, courses 20%, sync 25%.
- Money order: revenue in → hard costs → collaborators → Frass participation (Foundation allocation + Owner Compensation inside it) → creator's Frass Wallet.

## Label philosophy
- No advances, no debt, no ownership of people. "We're not buying you, we're building with you."
- People who join are **Vision Partners**, never "signed artists".
- One modular **Frass Vision Studios Creator Agreement**; only relevant modules apply.

## Divisions
FV Records (music), FV Films, FV Podcasts, FV Originals (TV), FV Publishing, FV Creative, FV Photo, FV Weddings, FV Documentaries (Foundation Stories — never published without Founder approval).

## Principle
Frass Vision Studios exists to build sustainable creative careers. We invest first in opportunity, technology, education and distribution rather than large signing advances. When creators succeed, Frass succeeds alongside them. Partnership, transparency, shared growth — not ownership of people.
