FRASS-0419 — **Frass Business Builder**, powered by Frass Vision Studios. Frass does not compete with Lovable/Bolt/v0 on prompts: those build websites, Frass builds **businesses** (site + marketplace + bookings + payments + brand kit + CRM + affiliate + email + analytics + SEO + Financial Center).

- Code: `src/lib/business-builder.ts`, route `src/routes/_authenticated/business-builder.tsx`.
- **One architecture, two doors**: a card in FV Studios (`/studio`) and a Business Builder item in the workspace sidebar + gateway nav. Never build a second builder.
- Two build paths: inside Frass (auto-wired to Marketplace, Wallet, Affiliate, Brand Partnerships, FV Studios, Radio, Daily, Financial Center) or independent/exportable site.
- 23 business types (not templates), 18 modular business systems, 13-item pre-launch Business Intelligence review, step-by-step one-question-at-a-time flow — never a wall of boxes.

FRASS-0419A — **Cost Constitution (platform-wide)**: Frass never silently absorbs third-party operating costs. Building/previewing is free; publishing, hosting, domains, premium AI, storage, SMS, email, streaming and CDN are paid by the customer at provider cost, with a modest transparent Frass service fee. Every cost, payee, recurrence and lower-cost alternative is shown before confirmation. No hidden charges, no silent subsidies.
