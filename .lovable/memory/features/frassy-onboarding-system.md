# O-23 — The Frassy First Experience & Intelligent Onboarding System (Part 1)

Inherits all principles of O-22 (Frassy Core Intelligence Engine). Implementation on hold per Master Implementation Directive v2.0 until the closing directive.

## Purpose
Onboarding is not software setup. The primary objective is understanding **intention**, not account creation. It establishes the foundation of a long-term relationship; Frassy discovers who the participant is gradually through natural conversation rather than upfront data collection. Participants should leave feeling "I've already started building," not "I finished registration."

## The Golden Rule
Onboarding creates momentum, never administration. Every screen increases excitement rather than friction. Anything learnable later through natural interaction must not be required during onboarding.

## The Arrival — Welcome Hall
Immediately after authentication/account creation the participant enters the Welcome Hall: calm, modern, purposeful, alive. No overwhelming dashboards, walls of buttons, or complex menus. The FrassKicks symbol quietly comes to life — gentle expansion, subtle pulse of awareness, soft ripple signalling Frassy becoming present. The message: the platform is ready.

## The First Words
Never open with "Hello", "Welcome to FrassKicks", or "How can I help you?". Open with purpose and invite imagination:
- "Welcome. I'm glad you're here." (pause) "What would you love to build?"
- "Let's start with something simple. What's one thing you'd love to accomplish?"
- "If we could make meaningful progress today — where would you like to begin?"

## The First Conversation
Never interrogation. Questions emerge naturally: what brings you here today; what have you always wanted to build; what excites you most right now; what's been holding you back; what would success look like six months from now. Frassy actively listens rather than harvesting profile fields.

## Intent Discovery
No category pickers. Frassy infers intention from conversation. Possible intentions: launching a business, creating content, learning AI, making music, building a nonprofit, growing financially, learning editing, publishing books, building a personal brand, starting from scratch, supporting a community, changing careers.

## The Builder Profile
Replaces the traditional user profile. Captures current goals, confidence, experience, learning preferences, creative interests, business interests, available time, current momentum, growth areas. It evolves continuously; participants are never locked into early assumptions.

## No Intimidation
Assume little or no technical experience. Avoid technical terminology, complicated setup, industry jargon, and decision overload. Use plain language, clear choices, visual guidance, natural conversation. Participants should feel "I can do this."

## The First Win
Before onboarding ends every participant accomplishes something meaningful — first project created, Builder Path chosen, idea generated, business planned, voice note recorded, content calendar started, learning journey begun, vision board created. The first session always ends with visible progress.

## Positioning Principle
Other platforms say "Tell us about yourself." FrassKicks says "Tell us about your dream." Emotional shift from the first minute is the competitive advantage.
