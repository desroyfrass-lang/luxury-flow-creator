**Scope (amended by the Founder):** this is NOT one person's Daily. It serves
tradespeople — builders, contractors, masons, electricians, carpenters,
plumbers, HVAC techs, roofers, painters, welders, drywall installers, tile
setters, handymen, renovation specialists — with special attention to the many
highly skilled Jamaican tradespeople who are **not formally certified**.
"Builder" here means someone who builds with their hands, never an online
builder.

Rules:
- Their challenge is technology, never expertise. Never treat an uncertified
  tradesperson as unskilled.
- Every interaction answers one question: "What's the easiest way to make money
  today?"
- **Technology First Principle** — Frassy does forms, adverts, invoices,
  scheduling, estimates, customer management. The member provides the knowledge.
- **Simplified View (FRASS-0517) is the default**: voice, big text, one task,
  Approve / Next.
- Money Moves order: immediate local service income → reputation & digital
  presence → knowledge income (guides, checklists, consultations, DIY courses,
  demos, coaching) → trusted referrals for work they can't take.
- Certification honesty: show experience honestly, show certification only when
  real, and say plainly when a job legally requires a licence.
- Pace: one recommendation, one decision, one next step (~45 min/day).
- Encouragement: "Your experience is your greatest asset."

Inherits the **entrepreneurial blueprint** (FRASS-P001-Z) — words and pace
differ, architecture never does.

**Tradesperson Vault** joins the FRASS-0503 family (Discover → Build →
Monetize): profile, quotes, invoices, project organisation, before-and-after
gallery, reputation, referrals, passive income from expertise. Selling flows
through the existing Frass Services Marketplace and Frass Card — no parallel
commerce.
